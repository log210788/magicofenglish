# Speaking the Magic ✨

A personalized executive presentation and communication roadmap for **Alessia**, Architect & Themed Entertainment Designer.

## Overview
This landing page provides:
- **The 8-Week Vocal & Emotional Progression**: Feeling Vocabulary ➔ Cadence & Flow ➔ Stress & Intonation ➔ The Vision Pitch.
- **Pre-Lesson Warm-Up**: A 2-minute voice reflection on *"What makes an experience feel truly magical?"* incorporating key feeling words (*Awe, Anticipation, Intimacy, Electric, Mysterious*).
- **In-Browser Audio Recorder**: Allows quick voice recording and downloading directly from the page.
- **Preply Submission Guide**: Clear, 3-step instructions for uploading audio notes before our sessions.
- **Lesson 1 Homework Portal** (`homework.html`): Interactive "Blueprint to Emotion" translation clinic, vocabulary precision quiz, audio walkthrough pitch recorder, and submission guidelines.
