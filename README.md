## 🧭 Why We Completely Redesigned the Course

### The Flaw of the Previous Approach (Oren Klaff / Wall Street Sales Frames)
The earlier curriculum attempted to synthesize Oren Klaff’s *Pitch Anything* ("croc brains," aggressive frame control, investor closing) with technical academic textbooks. For an architect with emerging English, this caused immediate friction:
1. **Hostile Sales Psychology Caused Cognitive Overload:** Non-native speakers cannot juggle aggressive boardroom combat games while simultaneously translating architectural thoughts into English. It triggered performance anxiety and unnatural posturing.
2. **Disconnected from Spatial Intuition:** Architects do not think in sales traps; they think in spaces, sightlines, light, textures, pacing, and guest emotion. Klaff’s framework suppressed Alessia's greatest natural strength: her architectural vision.

### The Imagineering Solution (The Kevin Rafferty Method)
In *Magic Journey*, legendary Disney Imagineer Kevin P. Rafferty (creator/writer of *Radiator Springs Racers, The Twilight Zone Tower of Terror, Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway, Test Track, Toy Story Mania!*) demonstrates how the world's most successful themed spaces are actually conceived and pitched:
1. **The Spatial Walkthrough Pitch:** Imagineers pitch by guiding the audience through the physical space:  
   *"Picture this: As guests step through the entrance portal... In Act 1, we decompress the guest... As the vehicle glides into Act 2, the lighting shifts... Suddenly, our signature reveal occurs!"*
2. **The 3-Act Attraction Architecture:** Every major Disney ride is structured like a three-act screenplay:
   - **Act 1:** Scenic Decompression & Environmental Immersion
   - **Act 2:** Theatrical Show-Controlled Darkness & Complication
   - **Act 3:** High-Speed Kinetic Payoff & Emotional Climax
3. **Marty Sklar’s Law:** *"Don't tell me you can't because... Tell me you can if!"*  
   Constraints (footprints, budgets, height limits) are solved through forced perspective, spatial compression, and bold creativity.
4. **Plussing Over Posturing:** Creative design reviews thrive on collaborative *"Yes, and how can we plus that?"* rather than ego defense.
5. **Multi-Sensory Environmental Language:** Grounding English in tangible sensory experiences: rockwork berms, verdigris patina, Smellitzer scent bursts, in-seat tactile effects, and trackless ride geometry.
