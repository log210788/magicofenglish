Photo 1 • The Landmark (El Weenie)

Willys Butte Peak

La montaña icónica al final del camino

monumental monumental, colosal

Meaning: Huge in scale, impressive size.

sun-bleached blanqueado por el sol

Meaning: Lightened in color by intense desert sun.

draws guests atrae a los visitantes

Meaning: Naturally pulls eyes and feet forward.

📸 Willys Butte attracts every visitor.
Photo 2 • Sightline Defense (El Berm)

The Perimeter Berm

El terraplén protector de roca y pinos

perimeter berm terraplén perimetral

Meaning: Earth wall built around the park edge.

sculpted rockwork roca esculpida

Meaning: Hand-crafted artificial rock layers.

hides the highway oculta la autopista

Meaning: Blocks outside traffic and city buildings.

📸 The rock berm protects immersion.
Photo 3 • Entrance Gateway (El Portal)

Rustic Canyon Portal

El portal de entrada rústico de madera

weathered timber madera envejecida

Meaning: Rustic wooden beams aged by rain and wind.

canyon portal portal del cañón

Meaning: An architectural entry arch framing the view.

opens up se abre de golpe

Meaning: Space dramatically expands after the arch.

📸 The timber arch frames the canyon.
