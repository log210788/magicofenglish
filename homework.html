Element 01 • Wayfinding

The Weenie

El Imán Visual / Faro Arquitectónico

Walt Disney’s term for an iconic, monumental visual anchor at the end of a sightline (like Willys Butte) that commands the horizon and guides guest flow forward.

En español: Estructura monumental que atrae la vista para que el visitante nunca se sienta perdido.
Element 02 • Sightline Defense

The Perimeter Berm

El Terraplén Perimetral Protector

An elevated rockwork ridge and landscaped earth mound wrapped around the boundary to block highways and outside city views, preserving complete thematic immersion.

En español: Barrera de tierra y roca esculpida que aísla el parque del mundo real exterior.
Element 03 • Sensory Threshold

Transition Portal

El Portal de Transición Sensorial

An architectural gateway (weathered timber archway) using spatial compression to transition guests from a busy arrival plaza into a sun-drenched canyon vista.

En español: Umbral arquitectónico que comprime y expande el espacio para transportar al huésped.

Part 4 • Structural Sentence Blueprints

The 4 Architectural Sentence Formulas

Estructuras para Alessia
Formula A: The Sightline Magnet (El Weenie) Orientación
[Spatial Location], [Architectural Element] [Spatial Action Verb] as a [Scale + Texture Adjectives] beacon, naturally [Circulation Verb] guest sightlines forward.
"Rising at the end of the central vista, Willys Butte commands the horizon as a monumental, sun-bleached beacon, naturally drawing guest sightlines forward."

Traducción: "Elevándose al final de la vista central, Willys Butte domina el horizonte como un faro monumental y blanqueado por el sol, atrayendo de forma natural la mirada del público."

Formula B: Immersion Defense (El Berm Perimetral) Protección Visual
In order to eliminate [Sightline Intrusion], we construct a [Dimension + Texture Adjectives] berm, which completely isolates [The Themed Land] from [Outside World].
"In order to eliminate sightline intrusion from the highway, we construct a 12-meter sculpted, striated rockwork berm, which completely isolates our desert land from the modern city."

Traducción: "Para eliminar la intrusión visual de la autopista, construimos un terraplén de roca esculpida y estriada de 12 metros, que aísla por completo nuestra tierra desértica de la ciudad exterior."

Formula C: Threshold Portal (Compresión y Expansión) Umbral Espacial
As guests pass beneath [Architectural Gateway], the space [dramatically expands / compresses], transitioning from [Atmosphere A] into [Atmosphere B].
"As guests pass beneath the rustic timber entry portal, the space dramatically expands, transitioning from the crowded arrival plaza into an amber-hued, sun-drenched canyon."

Traducción: "A medida que los visitantes cruzan el portal rústico de madera, el espacio se expande dramáticamente, pasando de la concurrida plaza de entrada a un cañón bañado por el sol con tonos ámbar."
