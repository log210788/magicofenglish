Part 1

The Feeling Vocabulary Vault

5 Core Pillars

01. Awe /ɔː/ (Noun)

Monumental Scale

The breathless, jaw-dropping feeling when you look up at something magnificent that overwhelms your senses.

✨ Designer Collocations:
"evokes sheer awe" • "jaw-dropping awe" • "steeped in awe"
Example: "As guests cross the threshold, the 18-meter atrium triggers an instant, collective gasp of awe."

02. Anticipation /ænˌtɪs.ɪˈpeɪ.ʃən/ (Noun)

The Build-Up

The excited butterflies in your stomach right before discovering what lies around the corner or behind the gate.

✨ Designer Collocations:
"palpable anticipation" • "heighten anticipation" • "pregnant with anticipation"
Example: "The winding, shadowed queue heightens the anticipation before the doors finally glide open."

03. Intimacy /ˈɪn.tɪ.mə.si/ (Noun)

Personal Scale

A warm, quiet, tucked-away quality that feels personal, cozy, protective, and human in scale.

✨ Designer Collocations:
"sense of intimacy" • "intimate sanctuary" • "enveloping intimacy"
Example: "We created an alcove bathed in amber lanterns to offer a rare pocket of intimacy amidst the bustling theme park."

04. Electric /iˈlek.trɪk/ (Adjective)

Kinetic Energy

A vibrant, humming kinetic energy in the air that makes your skin tingle with excitement and collective adrenaline.

✨ Designer Collocations:
"electric atmosphere" • "pulsing and electric" • "electric crescendo"
Example: "When the night spectacle ignites, the entire plaza becomes electric with music and dancing water."

05. Mysterious /mɪˈstɪə.ri.əs/ (Adjective)

Enigmatic Curiosity

A shadowy, intriguing atmosphere that conceals just enough to invite guests to step forward and uncover the secret.

✨ Designer Collocations:
"mysterious allure" • "shrouded and mysterious" • "invitingly mysterious"
Example: "The moss-covered stone archway feels deliciously mysterious, beckoning visitors to leave reality behind."

🏛️ The Spatial Transition Toolkit (Supporting Design Words)

Use these architectural storytelling terms alongside the feeling words to narrate client walkthroughs smoothly:

The Threshold The crossing point between the mundane outside world and the story within.
The Grand Reveal The choreographed sightline moment that delivers the emotional visual payoff.
Enveloping Surrounding all five senses so the guest is completely submerged in the environment.
Transportive Having the power to whisk someone into another reality or era.
Part 2

The "Blueprint to Emotion" Translation Clinic

Written Exercise

When an architect talks to an engineer, they speak in millimetres and fire ratings. But when pitching to a Creative Executive or Client, they must paint the picture of the human heart. Compare the transformations below, then try the practice challenge!

CASE STUDY 01: The Main Arrival Hall Target Emotion: AWE
❌ Blueprint Language (Dry / Technical):

"The entrance hall has a ceiling height of 16 meters with overhead curved trusses and skylights."

✨ Executive Pitch (Emotional Impact):

"The moment guests step inside, they are halted by a breathtaking sense of awe as soaring arches pull their gaze directly up toward the heavens."

CASE STUDY 02: The Queue & Pre-Show Tunnel Target Emotion: ANTICIPATION & MYSTERY
❌ Blueprint Language (Dry / Technical):

"Guests walk along an S-curved corridor with low ambient lux levels before reaching Door B."

✨ Executive Pitch (Emotional Impact):

"We deliberately carved a winding, mysterious passage that builds palpable anticipation with every step, leaving guests wondering what magic waits around the bend."

🎯 Your Interactive Practice Challenge

🎙️ Part 4 • The Main Speaking Deliverable

The 3-Minute Walkthrough Pitch

Record a 2 to 3-minute voice note presenting an experiential architectural walkthrough.

Your Speaking Brief:

Imagine you are walking an executive or client through an entrance sequence of your design (it can be an actual project, a themed pavilion, a fantasy hotel lobby, or a dream attraction).

Phase 1 (The Approach): How does the guest cross the threshold? What creates anticipation or makes it feel mysterious?

Phase 2 (The Grand Reveal): What is the sightline moment? What creates sheer awe or an electric atmosphere?

Phase 3 (The Sanctuary): Where can the guest retreat to feel warmth, quiet, and intimacy?

💡 Vocal Executive Technique:

Whenever you are about to say a feeling word ("awe", "anticipation", "intimacy"), take a tiny half-second breath pause right before it. That pause gives your voice weight and makes the listener lean in.
