The 8-Week Journey

4 Vocal Milestones
01

Feeling Vocabulary

Weeks 1–2

The words that sell our magic vision. Focusing on mood, sensation, and wonder—learning to describe what guests feel in their chest rather than what is built on paper.

02

Cadence & Flow

Weeks 3–4

How to say the words efficiently. Finding a calm, steady rhythm and using deliberate pauses so your audience has time to see the picture in their minds.

03

Stress & Intonation

Weeks 5–6

Giving the right feel to those words. Exploring vocal warmth, pitch variations, and emotional weight so your voice carries the mystery, scale, and excitement.

04

The Vision Pitch

Weeks 7–8

Putting it all together. Sharing your vision in a full walkthrough presentation with natural confidence, heart, and undeniable creative conviction.

✨ Pre-Lesson Warm-Up

"What makes an experience feel truly magical?"

Before our first lesson, record a relaxed 2-minute voice note answering this question. Forget blueprints, dimensions, or technical details—focus only on the feeling and the emotions.

Try weaving 2 or 3 of these feeling words into your note:

Awe

That breathless, jaw-dropping feeling when you look up at something magnificent.

Anticipation

The excited butterflies in your stomach right before discovering what's around the corner.

Intimacy

A warm, quiet, tucked-away space that feels personal, cozy, and safe.

Electric

A buzzing, vibrant energy in the air that makes your skin tingle with excitement.

Mysterious

A shadowy, curious atmosphere that invites you to step in and explore the unknown.
